Ask Captain

Just ask. Out loud.

Captain is the voice of BoatMedix. Ask about the weather, where to eat, what a channel marker means or who to call, and get a clear, spoken answer. It feels like a knowledgeable friend on board, not a chatbot.

Sample conversation. Shows how Captain is meant to work; answers in the app depend on your location and live data.

Is it going to storm this afternoon?
CAPTAINLooks like it. Storms are likely after 4 PM where you are. Want me to remind you at 3:30 to start heading in?
Yes. And is there anywhere to eat on the way back?
CAPTAINTwo places with docks between you and the ramp. One has live music at five. Want directions to either?
