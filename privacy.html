Privacy

Privacy notice. Plain and simple.

This notice explains what happens to the information you share when you join the BoatMedix waitlist or register for the Marine Pro Network on this website.

What we collect

  • Your first name and email address.
  • Your home lake or boating location, if you choose to share it.
  • Your answer to “What are you most excited about with BoatMedix?”, if you choose to share it.
  • Your choices about launch updates and public sharing, with the date and the wording you agreed to.

How we use it

  • To keep a list of people who want to hear when BoatMedix is ready, and to send you a one-time confirmation that you joined.
  • To send launch updates and occasional news, only if you ticked that box.
  • To show your first name, general location and comment on our website, only if you ticked that box and we have reviewed and approved your comment.
  • To share a total count of people on the waitlist. The count never identifies anyone.

Joining the waitlist is free. It doesn’t create a BoatMedix account, start a subscription or take any payment.

Marine Pro registrations

If you register at boatmedix.com/marinepro, we collect your business or professional name, contact details, location, specialties, experience and qualifications, and your consultation and service-area preferences. We use them only to build the Marine Pro Network and to contact you about it. Registering is free and doesn’t create a live listing, booking or payment. Your registration is never shown on the website.

Where it’s stored

Waitlist and registration information is stored in a private Google Workspace account controlled by BoatMedix LLC, which Google operates on our behalf. It is not stored in this website or its public code, and email addresses are never shown on the website. We do not sell your information.

Your choices

  • Stop launch updates at any time by replying “unsubscribe” to any email from us.
  • Ask us to remove your comment from the website, or to update or delete your information, by emailing hello@boatmedix.com.

How long we keep it

We keep this information until BoatMedix launches and for as long as it’s useful to tell you about BoatMedix, or until you ask us to delete it.

Questions

Email hello@boatmedix.com. BoatMedix LLC.
