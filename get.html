Good questions.

Is BoatMedix free?

Yes. The free app includes conditions, the map, Find Fuel, Find Help through the Marine Pro Network™ and nearby places. BoatMedix Plus will add Plan My Day, Captain and more when it opens.

Where does it work?

BoatMedix begins with the inland lakes we know best and is built to grow nationally, from small lakes to coastal waterways.

What is Captain?

Captain is the voice of the app. Ask a boating question out loud and get a clear, spoken answer about conditions, places or who to call.

Can I subscribe on the website?

Not yet. We’re designing one BoatMedix account across web and mobile, and we’ll open web subscriptions once those systems are built and tested.
