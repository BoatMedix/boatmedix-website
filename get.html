Good questions.

Is BoatMedix free?

There will be a free plan. It’s planned to include conditions, the map, Find Fuel, Find Help through the Marine Pro Network™ and nearby places. BoatMedix Plus will add Plan My Day, Captain and more when it opens.

Where does it work?

BoatMedix begins with the inland lakes we know best and is built to grow nationally, from small lakes to coastal waterways.

What is Captain?

Captain is the voice of the app. Ask a boating question out loud and get a clear, spoken answer about conditions, places or who to call.

Can I subscribe now?

Not yet. BoatMedix isn’t open to the public, and subscriptions aren’t available. Join the waitlist and we’ll let you know when it’s ready.

Does joining the waitlist cost anything?

No. Joining is free. It doesn’t start a subscription or take any payment, and you can ask us to remove you at any time.
