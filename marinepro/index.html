Captain, first

Captain makes the first call easier for everyone.

Captain is the connection between the boater and the network, so requests reach you with the basics already sorted.

Understands the problem

Captain asks the right questions and helps the boater describe what’s happening with their boat.

Starts with the basics

Where it’s appropriate, Captain walks through simple troubleshooting first. Some problems end right there.

Finds the right help

Captain helps the boater decide between a remote consultation and in-person service, then connects them with an available Marine Pro.

Safety comes before troubleshooting. If a situation could be dangerous, such as fire, flooding, injury or someone in the water, Captain is designed to point boaters to emergency help first: call 911, or hail the U.S. Coast Guard on VHF channel 16.

Trust, both ways

A marketplace built on accountability.

After a completed consultation or service, both sides can share how it went. Here’s how we plan to keep that fair.

Boaters rate their Marine Pro

  • Ratings and reviews come only from verified, completed interactions
  • Reviews are moderated for accuracy, relevance and respect
  • Marine Pros can respond, and can dispute a review they believe is unfair

Marine Pros share feedback too

  • Pros can give private feedback on their service experience with a boater
  • Boaters are never given public ratings that could expose personal information
  • Feedback is used to keep the network safe, not to unfairly restrict anyone’s access to help
  • A clear dispute process for both sides

Planned design. Ratings, reviews and disputes will be available once the network launches.

Good questions.

Is the Marine Pro Network live?

Not yet. We’re registering Marine Pros now. Consultations, bookings, dispatch and payments will open only after the service has been developed, tested and approved.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Registration is free while we build the network.

Do I have to be on call?

No. You decide when you’re available and which requests you accept. After-hours availability is optional.

Where does it work?

Everywhere in the United States. Remote consultations can connect you with boaters anywhere. For in-person work, you choose your own service area.

How will consultation rates and payment work?

We’re designing that with the Marine Pros who register. The plan is for professionals to set their own consultation rates. Nothing is charged or paid today.

Will you check qualifications?

Yes. We plan to verify Marine Pros before they can accept requests, so boaters can trust who they’re talking to. We’ll share the details with registered Marine Pros first.
