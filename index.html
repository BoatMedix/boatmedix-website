    What makes BoatMedix different

    Captain thinks ahead,
    so you don’t have to.

    Information used to be something you went looking for. BoatMedix brings the marine information that matters into one place: weather and radar, wind and water, official alerts, reports from other boaters, fishing rules, waterfront places, fuel and marine help. Captain, the AI built into BoatMedix, reads it all together and tells you what’s coming before you think to ask.
