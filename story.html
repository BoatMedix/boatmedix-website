Our story

The questions don’t stop at five o’clock.

BoatMedix comes from lived experience. I grew up around boating and a family deeply involved in the marine industry.

Over the years, friends and fellow boaters have asked for help finding marine services, choosing destinations, understanding conditions, and solving everyday boating problems.

Those questions don’t stop at five o’clock. They come on weekends, holidays, and right when someone wants to enjoy their boat.

So I started building the app I wished already existed. Something that brings the answers, places, and resources boaters need into one thoughtful experience.

Not to replace the joy of boating. To make more room for it.

BoatMedix is for people who love being on the water: sailors, anglers, pontoon owners, cruisers, first-time boaters and lifelong captains, on small inland lakes and along the coast. It begins with the lake we know best, and it’s built to go wherever the day takes you.

The voice in the app we call Captain. It’s named for my father, who answered most of those questions first. And if you ever notice a small Penny tucked into BoatMedix, that’s for the family dog who rode along on every one of those days.

Allison RobbFounder, BoatMedix
